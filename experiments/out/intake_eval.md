# Reading technicians' notes

88 notes, read on the demo day at 11:00. A reading is right only if the repair order, the part and
any new arrival time all match, or if a note that cannot be read comes out as unclear.

The first two columns flatter the rules reader: the same people wrote those notes and the rules. The last
column is the one to quote. Those notes were written by someone who saw only the day's jobs and parts, and
nothing is ever tuned on them.
